Experimental Nuclear Physics

Cross-sections, covariance and the physics that feeds nuclear data.

I measure neutron- and proton-induced reaction cross sections close to and above the Coulomb barrier, and work the resulting numbers into reaction networks for reactor materials and stellar nucleosynthesis — quantifying every uncertainty along the way with covariance analysis and unscented Kalman filtering.

Research interests

Reactor & astrophysical reactions

Reactions relevant to reactor materials, and nuclear reactions of astrophysical interest across the s-, r-, i- and p-processes.

Near-barrier reaction dynamics

Nuclear reactions close to the Coulomb barrier, and the resulting fusion / fission dynamics.

γ-ray spectrometry

Activation analysis and isotope identification using HPGe and scintillation detector systems.

Thermonuclear reaction rates

Building reaction rates for use in stellar nucleosynthesis and reaction-network codes.

Uncertainty quantification

Parameter estimation with the unscented Kalman filter, and full covariance analysis of measured cross sections.

Bayesian statistics & networks

Bayesian inference applied to nuclear data evaluation and nuclear reaction network modelling.
